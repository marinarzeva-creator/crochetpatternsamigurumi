Frequently Asked Questions

What is amigurumi and is it suitable for beginners?
Amigurumi is the Japanese art of crocheting small stuffed yarn creatures and plushies. Many amigurumi patterns use basic stitches like single crochet worked in continuous rounds, making them very accessible for enthusiastic beginners with basic crochet knowledge.

What yarn and tools do I need to start crocheting plushies?
To get started with amigurumi, you will typically need medium-weight cotton or plush acrylic/chenille yarn, a small crochet hook (usually 2.5mm–4.0mm to keep stitches tight), polyester fiberfill stuffing, safety eyes, and a tapestry needle.
